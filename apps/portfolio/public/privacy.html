Privacy and analytics

This portfolio keeps cookie-free aggregate counts of page views, referring hostnames and outgoing link destinations. The private dashboard stores these aggregates for up to 90 days. Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control are respected.

Optional Google Analytics

When configured, Google Analytics runs on the portfolio homepage only after you choose “Allow Google Analytics”. Until then, this site does not load Google’s analytics tag. You can change your choice using “Analytics settings” in the footer. Declining keeps the portfolio usable.

With permission, Google Analytics receives page views, browser/device information and outgoing destination hostnames. Google may derive approximate location from network information. Analytics cookies are configured for up to 90 days; your consent choice stays in this browser. Advertising features and Google signals are disabled in the site’s configuration. Reporting retention is controlled by the site owner’s Google Analytics settings.

The site’s manually configured events exclude contact-form contents, email addresses, app inputs and URL query strings/fragments. Developer tools and private account areas do not load this homepage tag. See Google’s Privacy Policy.

Contact and accounts

Information you submit through the contact form is used to respond to your enquiry. Shared-account services store the information needed for sign-in and the projects or experiments you save. Do not include secrets in public experiment links.

For privacy questions, use the contact form.
